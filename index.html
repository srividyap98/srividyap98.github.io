Research

Research in progress at the intersection of agentic AI and production reliability.

Working Paper · Agentic AI · AIOps · Trustworthy ML

Risk-Gated Evidence-Verified Multi-Agent Incident Remediation with Selective Human Oversight

This work studies selective autonomy in LLM-based incident remediation: when should an AI-generated remediation be trusted, and when should the system escalate to a human because operational risk is high or retrieved evidence does not sufficiently support the proposed action?

Retrieve Evidence → Build Context → Assess Incident Risk → Generate Remediation → Verify Claims → Auto-Resolve or Escalate

Research questions: whether combined risk and grounding gates reduce unsafe or unsupported remediation; whether calibrated escalation improves the safety-versus-automation trade-off; whether hierarchical retrieval improves evidence grounding; and how the system behaves under missing, irrelevant, contradictory, or misleading evidence.

Experimental design: compare a direct LLM, single-agent RAG, ungated multi-agent RAG, risk-only gating, grounding-only gating, and the proposed dual-gated system using paired evaluation and controlled ablations.

Evaluation: root-cause accuracy, remediation correctness, unsafe-remediation rate, unsupported claims, human-escalation rate, safe automation coverage, calibration, latency, token usage, and inference cost. Quantitative claims will be added only after the evaluation protocol is frozen and experiments are executed.
